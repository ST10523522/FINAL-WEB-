ABOUT THE BOUYZ

LOCAL BRAND EST 2023 | HATFIELD PRETORIA

Our Story


We are students from Hatfield, Pretoria. In 2023 we noticed Diesel jackets cost R3000 in Menlyn Mall. Most students can't afford that. So we started The Bouyz - Diesel-inspired designs made locally for R299-R699. Same drip, local price. Same quality, affordable.



Mission and Vision


Make trendy streetwear accessible to all South African youth. Support local printing, local models, local culture. We believe local is lekker, affordable and trendy.



Why Choose Us?


  • Local brand from Pretoria - supports local economy
  • Affordable prices from R299 - student friendly
  • Premium quality - Diesel-inspired designs
  • Fast delivery - Pretoria same day
